This study investigates the mechanisms responsible for heating the solar corona by analyzing the frequency distribution of solar flares.

Using more than 600 individual flare case studies, the authors determined a power-law exponent of \(\alpha = 1.63 \pm 0.03\). Since this is below the critical threshold of \(\alpha = 2\), the results suggest that Alfvén waves play an important role in coronal heating.
